The Cosmological Map · Concentric View
The same order, drawn as it actually nests. At the core, three intersecting circles share one plane — Formal Framework, the Mortal Layer, and Hell Faction — the Framework and Hell opposing each other across the world between them. Every circle beyond encompasses what it governs.

Read it from the center out: the Web holds the middle and reaches into both sides; the Bone Mother’s Realm holds more; the Corridor rings the Mortal Layer in gold; the Grigori encircle nearly all three; and the Older Mechanism, the Arcana, the Veil, the Abyss, and the Unwritten close around everything in turn.

Along the bottom of each ring runs its planetary correspondence — outward from Earth (the Web, the living world) through Mars (the Bone Mother’s realm of the dead), Jupiter (the Grigori host), Saturn (the Older Mechanism’s law and time), Uranus (the primordial Arcana), Neptune (the Veil’s dissolution and threshold), Pluto (the Abyss, the underworld), and out to the Oort Cloud at the edge of everything (the Unwritten).

The Frequency Principle · The Foundational Law of All Magical Work
The frequency of the body changes during high emotional vibrations.
The stronger the emotion — the stronger the frequency — the stronger the work.

This is not a principle of dark arcana alone. It is the foundational operating law of every magical system in the H&H universe. The daughters’ practice harnesses it through love, trust, and the correct bond. Dark arcana harnesses it through fear, grief, and forced emotional extremity. Same law. Opposite application.
Frequency Doctrine
The Death Frequency
Every body releases a specific frequency at the moment of death. This is how Hecate knows — she reads it and opens the passage to the Bone Mother’s realm, which exists just behind the Veil before the soul reaches Hell. Vine (Duke, formerly a mortal witch-hunter) discovered through dark arcana that this frequency can be harnessed. His secret: the death frequency is exponentially stronger when death occurs during high emotional states, particularly fear. A body that dies in fear releases a signal many times more powerful than a body that dies at peace. Vine brought this to Lucifer as the power source for the seal.
Frequency Doctrine
Emotion as Amplifier
The principle applies to all magical work. A practitioner working through love channels a different frequency than one working through fear — neither breaks the law, both use it. The bond’s frequency is the most resilient because love freely given and freely received produces the longest-held emotional vibration in the human body. Twenty-six years of Carla June’s warm room is an extraordinary frequency source. That is why dark arcana cannot corrupt it: you cannot disrupt a frequency that is being continuously replenished by the same emotion that constitutes it.
Frequency Doctrine
The Moon Phases · Demonic Operational Windows
Demons work best in shadows. Hecate’s vigil is the mortal layer’s primary protection. The Full Moon: worst night for demonic activity. Hecate’s attention is at maximum. Her frequency is at its peak. The New Moon: peak demonic activity. Hecate’s attention is at its most inward. Every significant demonic operation in the universe’s history has been timed to the new moon when possible. Vine’s Church persecution served two purposes simultaneously: the deaths in fear produced massive frequency harvests, and Hecate’s grief at watching her mother’s daughters persecuted caused her to turn from her vigil — lowering the Veil’s average frequency across all moon phases over centuries. The world has only gotten darker since.

In life Abaddon was a dragon slayer. Vine (Duke, formerly a mortal witch-hunter who discovered the death-frequency principle) brought Lucifer the idea of the Church: harvest fear-death frequency, turn Hecate from her vigil through grief at her mother’s daughters being persecuted, and suppress the daughters’ lateral connections. The Dark Ages were the direct consequence. The world has only gotten darker since.
Satan & The Two Sealings
Satan — The Red Dragon
His original form was the massive Red Dragon. He directed Cain to kill Arcadia for choosing the Leviathan over him. Abaddon slew the dragon at Arcadia’s grave — and the frequency of that grief-battle reached Sano, which is where the deal was made. The Bone Mother rejected Satan’s stained soul; he entered Hell as the Prince of Wrath, more dangerous than Lucifer.
The Wrong Compact & OS5
First sealing: after Abaddon walked past him in Hell’s streets like he was nothing, the confrontation required the wrong compact’s fear-substrate to contain him — his prison lock, maintained 12,000 years. OS5 is the second and permanent sealing, into the ruins of the Vatican Palace, before his full power could resurrect the dragon.
The OS5 → DA1 Mechanism
Lucifer’s Full-Knowledge Sacrifice
He gave himself up knowing what it would cost him — not Nepisano’s plan, but his own price — to finally make right his two murders and seal Satan before the dragon could return. His reward: he was brought to the Arcana Layer, the first condemned soul ever received upward.
The Tower’s Shock
The Tower’s structural role is GUARD. When Lucifer was elevated, both Lucifer and the Tower were shocked at once — a condemned soul received upward, shared wonder on both sides. That mutual wonder disrupted the Tower’s guard for a moment. Hecate had already turned her full gaze to the world (the Moon went suddenly full, overcharging the seal); Nepisano grabbed her hand in the gap. Abaddon’s deal covered Nepisano approaching her — not her turning willingly. The loophole.
The Seven-Year Cycle
Fire Solstice, Year 0 → Year 7
The compact opens on the fire solstice in Year 0 — the longest day, the sun at its peak. Seven years later, on the same solar position, the Restoration closes the cycle. The Older Mechanism’s record spans the full arc.
