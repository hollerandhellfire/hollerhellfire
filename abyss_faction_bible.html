THE LAYER

It is the one place in creation Sano’s Light was never asked to reach — not dark the way night is dark, but dark the way a thing with no frequency at all is dark.
The Layer · A Region of Its Own
Where It Sits
The Second Layer · The Oort Cloud, Beneath the Unwritten
In the cosmological stack of eleven layers, the Abyss is the second layer — directly beneath The Unwritten (where Nepisano resides at the saga’s start) and directly above The Veil. It is the Oort Cloud, the outermost layer of Sano’s Creation and the edge where Creation stops. The Unwritten lies beyond it, outside Creation: the space between galaxies. The Abyss is not a sphere, not a territory, not a world anyone governs. It is a region of creation in its own right — a cosmic layer, a place with a location on the map of everything, sitting at the cold floor beneath the Unwritten and the ceiling above the Veil that protects the mortal order below.
What It Is Not
Not Pluto · Not Hell · Not the Door
The Abyss must not be confused with the places near it. It is not Pluto — that is the frozen world the Davos took, and the Davos are its wardens, not its contents. Pluto lies inside the system; the Oort Cloud lies far beyond it. It is not Hell — Hell is a parallel realm built from Nepisano’s wounded mind, far below in the stack beside the Formal Framework. It is not the doorway Nepisano’s insane energy tore. The Abyss is older and emptier than any of them — the layer the lost fall through on the way to becoming nothing, and the layer where the things that should be nothing collect instead.
The Character of the Place
The Light of Nothing
Where the Veil below is a membrane and the Unwritten above is potential not yet spoken, the Abyss between them is subtraction given a place to happen. It is colorless — not dark the way night is dark, but dark the way a thing with no frequency at all is dark. Sound does not carry correctly. Direction does not hold. Nothing here resonates, because resonance requires relationship, and the Abyss is where relationship has failed so completely that even the broken frequencies have gone quiet. It is the one place in creation Sano’s Light was never asked to reach.

THE LEFTOVER

Everything in the Abyss shares one wrongness: it continues when it should not. A ruin is over; nothing in the Abyss is over.
The Leftover · What the Abyss Keeps
The Core Truth
Leftover Creation · Six Failed Cycles
The Abyss is filled with the leftover creation of every failed cycle. Six times before the current one, creation ran its course and reset — and six times, things that were made in those cycles should have ceased when the cycle did, and did not. They continued to exist, orphaned, with no place in the cycle that followed. The Abyss is where they gathered: the remnants of six endings, persisting past the worlds that made them, belonging to no present and no governance. Only the Bone Mother knew the cycles happened at all; only she could know what the Abyss truly holds.
The Second Population
The Stranded Souls
The leftovers are not alone. Per the oldest telling, souls who find no place ‘wander the darkness and end up in the Abyss’ — the souls left unplaced because the Bone Mother’s Gate is structurally closed while a Cycle is unresolved — not a moral rejection of anyone. With Heaven and Hell already overrun and the Gates shut, the unplaced drift, and some drift here. The Abyss is the catch-basin of creation — the failed-cycle remnants from above the floor of time, and the stranded souls from within it, meeting in the one layer that was never meant to hold anyone.
Why They Persist
Continuity Without a World
Everything in the Abyss shares one wrongness: it continues when it should not. A remnant of a dead cycle has no world to belong to; a stranded soul has no Gate to pass and no realm to settle. Both are continuity cut loose from the thing that should have ended or placed it. This is the Abyss’s single law — persistence without a present to persist into. It is why the layer is a dread and not merely a ruin: a ruin is over, and nothing in the Abyss is over. It simply keeps on, in the cold, with nowhere to keep on toward.

INHABITANTS

Inhabitants · The Unplaced
Two populations and one named terror. What is fixed: the Abyss holds the leftover creation of six failed cycles and the stranded souls. Specific entities beyond the Serpent are reserved for development.
The Remnants
The Failed-Cycle Leftovers
Creations — beasts, forms, half-finished makings — from the six cycles that came before. Each was native to a world that no longer exists. Shapeless or monstrous, they are defined by not fitting: nothing in the current cycle is built to the frequency they were made in. The oldest and largest among them are the deepest dread in the layer.
The Stranded
The Gateless Souls
Souls left unplaced because the Bone Mother’s Gate stands structurally closed — she is not a judge and rejected no one — who wandered until the darkness took them in. Not damned (that is Hell) and not elevated (that is Heaven) — simply unplaced, drifting in a layer with no order to sort them. They lose coherence slowly, the way the Ferals of Hell lose their humanity, but with nothing to become instead.
The Abyss Serpent
The Thing That Wakes First
The single named terror of the layer — the largest, oldest remnant, a serpent out of a cycle so far back only the Bone Mother remembers its world. It is the first thing to stir when the Abyss stirs. Reserved: its strike opens the Seventh Cycle (SC1), when the failing order lets the layer’s contents spill toward creation for the first time.

THE VIGIL

The whole arrangement is a holding, not a cure. There is no cure for continuity that refuses to cease.
The Vigil · What Holds It Back
The One Who Holds It
Abaddon · Guardian of the Abyss
The Abyss has one guardian, and it is Abaddon. The human who slew Satan’s dragon form, crossed to the Arcana Layer in death, and struck a deal with Sano — he keeps the vigil at the cold edge of creation, and the deal also requires him to bring Nepisano back whole — but not before then. He is in Hell by the terms of the deal, which ties him to Hell without making him part of it; the cold that corrupts everything it touches never corrupted him, because he is there by a chosen bond, not a fall. What Abaddon stands beside, at its deepest, is everything the Abyss has kept.
The Second Line
The Davos as Wardens
Abaddon holds the layer back; the Davos are the Bars. On frozen Pluto, as far from Sano’s Light as the spheres allow, the 12 Davos are the warden-monsters of the Abyss — the cell-wall set against what the layer contains. They are not the Abyss’s leftover creatures and never were; they are the original corrupted, ranked by ruthlessness, who ward the pit without being its contents. (See the Davos faction bible. Where the two overlap, the Davos are the keepers; the Abyss is the kept.)
Why It Holds
Containment of the Unending
The Abyss is held because its contents cannot be ended — only kept. A remnant with no world cannot be killed back into a world that is gone; a gateless soul cannot be sorted by Gates that are shut. So the layer is not sealed shut so much as watched — Abaddon’s vigil at the edge, the Davos’s bars at Pluto, and above it all the Bone Mother’s threshold-order, within which even the Abyss is a thing contained and not a thing loose. The whole arrangement is a holding, not a cure. There is no cure for continuity that refuses to cease.
The Thesis Underneath
The Opposite of the Bond
The Abyss is the cosmology’s quiet argument stated in the negative. Everything the series is built on — relationship, chosen and test-proven, trust and belief as verbs — is exactly what the Abyss has none of. It is the layer where relationship failed so utterly that its contents could neither resolve nor rest. Accumulated love is the counter to accumulated corruption; the Abyss is what the absence of that love looks like when it is given a place and left there for six cycles. The Restoration is built bond by bond because the Abyss is the proof of what the alternative keeps.

STANDING CANON

Standing Canon · Fixed & Reserved
Locked Canon
What Is Fixed
The Abyss is the second layer of the cosmological stack (The Unwritten → Abyss → The Veil → …): its own cosmic region, not Pluto, not Hell, not the doorway. It is the Oort Cloud, the outermost layer of Sano’s Creation; the Unwritten lies beyond it, outside Creation, in the space between galaxies. It holds the leftover creation of the six prior failed cycles — things that should have ceased at each reset and persisted — and the souls left unplaced by the structurally closed Gate, who wander the darkness and end up there. Its single law: continuity without a world to belong to. Its guardian is Abaddon, who stands beside it by the deal — a witness on the only road out of the deepest Unwritten, and a model of standing beside severance without becoming it. The Abyss is bounded, not destroyed, and it is distinct from Hell. The Davos (Pluto) are its wardens / Bars, not its contents. These points are fixed by The Origin (the cosmogony) and The Codex, and by the Pre-Creation Bible for the Oort Cloud and the Unwritten.
Open / Reserved
Held for Later
The Abyss Serpent is named as the layer’s oldest remnant and its first-to-wake; it is named as one of the compensating responses in the Seventh Cycle (SC1), and the Abyss stays a dormant dread/location through the earlier arcs until then. (The Master Outline attributes SC1 to the Gate opening; the Serpent’s exact role is pending the Abyss / Abaddon placement audit.) The specific remnants of each prior cycle, the individual stranded souls, and any Abyss creature that steps onto the page beyond the Serpent are reserved for development. How far the layer’s contents spill once the order fails in the Seventh Cycle is placed in that arc’s bible, not here.
Master Outline
Master Outline Boundary Locks
The Abyss remains distinct from Hell, and the Unwritten remains distinct from corruption; both are bounded, not destroyed (SC12). Abaddon is the Abyss guardian by deal — a witness on the only road out of the deepest Unwritten who stands beside severance without becoming it. The Abyss / Abaddon final placement audit (the exact road between the Unwritten and Creation, “bring him back whole,” and witness without judgment) is still to be completed. Abaddon enters the Divine Arc directly at DA11; the Abyss answers at the end of DA12.
