PREMISE

The Arc in Context
The compact has been solid for a year. The Pike County corridor holds. The anchor work is maintenance.

What happens in the Expansion Arc is not a crisis — it is recognition. The correct compact's frequency is attracting the practitioners who belong in it, in corridors across the eastern seaboard and beyond. New Watcher bonds, new Web threads, new corridors entering the Ley Line network — twelve books in all.

The Wardens, in the deep places, begin to feel the gathering.
Why This Arc Exists
The Leviathan Cannot Wake Into a Single Corridor
The New Compact — ratified at WMB12 by 31 correctly placed, freely chosen threads — requires the Web's frequency to be sufficiently constituted before its authority can hold. EA12 (Every Corridor Counted) carries the first thread-count assessment. The Expansion Arc is the arc that gets the count from the threads placed in Books 1–6G toward the 31 the New Compact needs.

Each Expansion Arc corridor adds a thread. Each bond is a new node in the Ley Line network. Each corridor's practitioner adds their specific gift to the Web's frequency.

The Leviathan is feeling this. It cannot yet speak. But every new thread placed is felt in the deep position.
The Arc's Most Significant Contribution
D Coale Builds the Instrument
The atmospheric monitoring layer D establishes at Penn State is the most structurally significant gift in the arc. She does not know this yet. She is running models.

What her instruments support:
• The first thread-count assessment at EA12 (the New Compact itself is ratified at WMB12)
• Map the Warden communication medium — the Ley Lines expressed as weather
• Register the unexplained upper-frequency reflection boundary that foreshadows Sano’s Barrier (EA4)

She is running the models. She will run them forty times before she is sure.
The Archive Connection
Penemue Has Been Carrying the First Word for Twelve Thousand Years
Penemue carries every word ever written. Among those words is Arcadia's first word — here — written in the soil above the Leviathan's heart twelve thousand years ago and rendered into the written record through the geological inscription's translation.

He has carried it without knowing why it felt different from everything else in the archive. The bond with Emily Keldren in the Catskill Valley — the written tradition anchor — is the moment the archive's connection to the Leviathan Arc becomes accessible to the network.

Brian Lee will mediate a rock dragon in Emily's corridor in WMB4. The Catskill corridor is the first evidence of how far the Leviathan's geological range extends.

The Ley Line Network — How It Grows
Thread Count Progress
From 6 Threads to Sufficient
At the start of the Expansion Arc, the network holds 6 correctly placed threads from Arc I — the Pike County primary plus five supporting threads from the initial H&H bondings.

The Expansion Arc adds a thread with each new corridor bond. By EA12, the first thread-count assessment is made; the count is moving toward the 31 that will ratify the New Compact at WMB12.

Three of Ravos's seventeen thread locations in WMB1 are at secondary Ley Line nodes between the Expansion Arc's corridors — meaning the Expansion Arc's thread placements are precisely where the Leviathan Arc's geological work needs them.

D doesn't know this yet. She is mapping the atmospheric layer. She will run the models forty times. The assessment at EA12 is only a first count — the compact itself waits for WMB12.
The Warden Communication Medium
Why D Coale Can Hear Them
The Wardens communicate through the Ley Lines' surface expression — the atmospheric weather patterns that run along the routes the Ley Lines follow. D's atmospheric monitoring at Penn State maps these patterns with scientific precision, which means she is, without fully understanding it, mapping Warden communications.

When Tharael of the Alps begins to detect the North American signal, D's instruments register an anomalous high-pressure system over the Atlantic that has no meteorological origin. She flags it in her documentation and keeps running the models.

She will run the models for a long time before she understands what she's been hearing.

TONE & CANON

Tone Notes
Each book is a new kind of knowing
The Expansion books are not repeating the Pike County model — each corridor has its own practice, its own gift, its own specific relationship to the geological and atmospheric layers. The expansion is not replication. It is the Web discovering that the correct practice looks different in a delta than in a coal holler.
The Wardens are in the background
No Warden speaks directly in the Expansion Arc books. But their stirring is felt — in the specific way D's atmospheric readings begin to show patterns that have no meteorological explanation, in the way the WV Thread placement produces a tremor Abigail has no geological precedent for, in the way Penemue pauses over one specific entry in the archive before he fully understands why.
The network is not imposed — it assembles
None of the heroines are recruited to a cause. They arrive at their practice through their own histories, their own gifts, their own corridors. The network assembles because the correct compact's frequency attracts the practitioners who belong in it. This is the daughters' tradition's specific character: you don't build the Web. You find that it was always there.
D Coale is the arc's most structurally significant character
She is building instruments, not performing practice. Her atmospheric monitoring is the arc's most consequential gift to the universe — her instruments contribute to the first thread-count assessment (EA12), register the upper-frequency reflection boundary that foreshadows Sano’s Barrier, and map the Warden communication medium. She doesn't know any of this yet. She is running the models.
Permanent Canon
The network expands from a Pike County-specific operation to a multi-corridor web across the twelve Expansion books (EA1–EA12) without annexation — joining the Network is not annexation.
Each new corridor adds its Web thread to the Ley Line network — the thread count grows toward the 31 correctly placed, freely chosen threads that ratify the New Compact at WMB12. EA12 holds the first thread-count assessment.
The WV Thread (EA3) is the first corridor thread the Leviathan can feel from the geological depth — the first evidence from outside Pike County that the Web is being correctly constituted.
D Coale's atmospheric monitoring (EA4) is network instrumentation; its first anomaly is an unexplained upper-frequency reflection boundary that foreshadows Sano’s Barrier.
Penemue's archive (EA5) carries Arcadia's first word — here — among every word ever written. The significance of this becomes legible in WMB4 when Brian Lee mediates the Catskill rock dragon in the corridor Emily holds.
Tharael (Alps) and Ilavar (Rockies) begin to detect the North American Ley Line signal strengthening during the Expansion Arc — the first Warden awareness of the network's growth beyond the Appalachian primary.
ARC II LOCKS (Master Outline): Corridors are relational ecologies, not power nodes. Joining the Network is not annexation. Measurement never overrides personhood. A map is not a deed. Handoff: something is responding from below, and the Network chooses to listen.
The Expansion Arc's corridors establish the global network that the Leviathan Arc, the Web of Weavers Arc, and the Divine Arc will build upon. The gathering that begins here makes everything else possible.
