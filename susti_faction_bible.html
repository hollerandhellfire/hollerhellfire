Before Territory
The Bang · Gaia’s Handmaidens
At the Bang — when Sano pulled the space-dust together into ten spheres inside a huge eleventh — the assembled factions came to awareness. Among them were the Susti: the 7 Untouched, Gaia’s handmaidens. Where the Davos are the original corrupted, the Susti are their exact opposite: the original uncorrupted. They are the virgin handmaidens of Gaia and the attendants of her Garden — seven, the Seed of Life’s generative count, one for each continent.
The Untouched
Never Severed From the Origin
The defining fact of the Susti: a Susti carries no corruption. She was never severed from the origin frequency, never touched by the static — hence the Untouched. This is why they alone can make accurate records and teach true survival: there is no distortion in them to pass on. They are the clean signal in living form, the proof that a frequency can exist in creation entirely uncorrupted. Everything they make and teach is accurate because its maker is accurate.
Territory
Earth · Gaia’s Garden
The Susti took no separate sphere of their own. Gaia took Earth for her Garden, and the Susti maintain it for her — one keeper to each continent. They are the only faction whose home is Gaia’s own world, because their entire purpose is the Garden’s keeping. They dwell, like all the founding factions, on the Arcana Layer, but their work is here, in the living world: tending the Garden, and — when humanity came — teaching the first people how to live in it.

The Two Teachings
The Charge
Sent Not to Serve But to Survive
When Gaia created the first humans — seven pairs in all — each mated pair was sent, pair by pair, to a Susti governess. CRITICAL: the pairs were sent not to SERVE but to SURVIVE. The first humans could not learn to hold their frequency in the Mortal Layer alone — not in a sphere where the corruption seed was already sealed inside. They needed an untouched guide to teach them to exist at full frequency. This is the foundational teacher-student relationship of the entire human story.
First Teaching
Survival of the Land
Each Susti taught her pair how to survive on the specific continent that would be their home to tame — its conditions, its dangers, its frequency-character. If a pair took root, more pairs were later sent to help populate the area. This is the origin of human geographic and cultural diversity: different continents, different survival-knowledge, different lifeways, all branching from one seven-pair origin under seven untouched teachers. The first teaching is the root of why humanity is many.
Second Teaching
The Power of Frequency Tuned Right
The deeper teaching — the central thesis of the entire universe, taught at the origin of humanity by beings who had witnessed it firsthand: when a relationship is built on trust that is test-proven, and belief in proven truth is the foundation of the verb love, with acceptance as the resonance of the frequency, then anything is possible. This is not philosophy layered on afterward; it is the literal first lesson taught to humanity by the Untouched. The second teaching is the root of why humanity is, at its best, one.
The Proof
What the Susti Witnessed
The Susti taught the second teaching because they had seen it with their own eyes — in the love of Sano and Gaia. When Sano first met Gaia, his resonance with her frequency allowed him to create a stable universe from space dust. The Bang was not raw power — it was the product of a relationship. The Susti witnessed the foundational proof that relationship built correctly is the most creative force that exists, and they taught it to the first humans. The Indigenous traditions worldwide that stayed closest to it — the land is alive, the human woven into the web, right relationship the fundamental work — preserved the second teaching most faithfully.
The First Teacher
The Original Bond
The First Teacher-Student Relationship
The Susti-and-pair relationship is the original of every later master-student bond. The path to surviving at full frequency has always run through one specific relationship with one specific untouched guide who recognizes the student and teaches them to hold what they are. Every teacher-lineage in human spiritual history is a descendant of this first arrangement: the Sufi murshid and seeker, Rumi and Shams, the Kabbalistic teacher and student, the Calhoun succession passing the practice down.
Patience and Arcadia
The Line That Produced the First OMNI
The clearest fruit of the Susti’s teaching: Patience, governess of the Asiatic continent, trained Akin and Lestile — and from their line came Arcadia, the first OMNI Practitioner, the only early human able to receive any frequency and translate it correctly on every level. Because she could, she saw straight through Satan’s corruption at Babel and turned him down flat — the first Calhoun woman. The whole Calhoun succession, down to Edna Mae and Dixie, descends from Patience’s teaching.
Why It Matters Now
The Untouched Guide in Every Arc
The Susti’s teaching-pattern is the template the entire saga runs on. Every practitioner who learns to hold her frequency does so through a recognizing guide; every correct bond is an echo of the first one. When the Restoration is built bond by bond, it is the Susti’s second teaching being proven true again at scale — the central thesis the Untouched carried from the love of Sano and Gaia all the way down to a woman on a road choosing to believe a fallen thing.
Nguvu’s Map
The Manuscript
Nguvu’s Original Map
Nguvu, the Susti of Africa, created the original intact map of the cosmological architecture — the only fully accurate record of the Network’s structure ever made in the Mortal Layer. It is accurate because its maker was untouched. Human tradition later knew it, in distorted form, as the Kabbalistic Tree of Life. Every Tree-of-Life teaching humanity has is a partial, copied-down echo of Nguvu’s original — the spheres, the pathways, the map of both the cosmos and the soul’s ascent.
The Lineage
Down to David and Solomon
The manuscript passed down the line of the original African pair Awalaan and Zawja, through the nomad peoples, to a land they were certain their God had chosen. King David was the closest to the Enlightened State of his line; his son Solomon was the second closest — but was taken by corruption when he created the Seals of Solomon. The problem with a seal: it works both ways — it does not close a door, it makes a hallway between two doors. Solomon is the cautionary case of an almost-Enlightened practitioner operating under distortion.
Why It Holds
The Untouched Maker
Nguvu’s map could not be fully corrupted because of how the Written Word works: a hard copy carries the frequency of its maker, and Nguvu’s frequency was untouched. Translation into the corruption’s tongues distorts it, copying degrades it — but the original carries the clean signal of an uncorrupted hand. This is the Susti’s deepest gift to humanity: a true map, made by a true maker, durable enough to survive thousands of years of distortion and still point at what is real.
