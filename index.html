A Universe Where Heaven Gets Rewritten From a Holler

The Holler & Hellfire Universe is a single, living world told two ways: through the paranormal romance novels of Kaylin Renea, and through the outlaw country rap of Dixie Deadshot on Holler & Hellfire Records.

Eleven books. Seventy-five songs. Fallen Watchers and the women who anchor them. Every record is a book’s companion; every book is a bond. Welcome to the Thinning.
