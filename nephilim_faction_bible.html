OVERVIEW

What This File Is
This is a working bible. The number of Nephilim known in the main series is small because Brian Lee Combs and Wren Tackett have not grown up enough in the main series to find the rest. That story belongs to its own series, later.

Everything confirmed lives here, everything provisional is marked, and everything unknown is left as an empty slot on purpose.
Doctrine
Human Beings Who Carry a Watcher's Frequency in Their Bodies
The classical record calls the Nephilim giants and fallen ones, the children of the sons of God and the daughters of men. The H&H universe calls them the first new thing in the world since the founding: people who can translate between the geological scale and the human scale, who are neither Watcher nor practitioner, and for whom the Older Mechanism has no previous category.
Where They Sit in the Stack
Between the Grigori and the Mortal Layer
Not a layer of their own. A frequency that sits between two layers of the cosmological stack, so a Nephilim does not so much live in one as stand in both. Anything about how they relate to the Veil, the Corridor and the Web is a development question.
Status Key

The Series Thesis, Applied
A relationship built on trust and belief.

A Nephilim is what happens when two beings from different scales choose each other and the choice produces a person. They are the thesis with a face.

NATURE · THE IN-BETWEEN

The Nature of the In-Between
Frequency
Neither Human Nor Watcher
A Nephilim operates on a frequency that is neither human nor Watcher: the in-between. That is why Brian Lee reads geological frequency at human-accessible scale. His native frequency sits between scales. He hears his parents' bond natively, where a Hell Prince hears only music in a language he cannot parse, because he is the proof of that bond.
Function
The Connection, Not the Tool
A Nephilim does not use a bridge. A Nephilim is one. Brian Lee, in his own words: <em>I think I'm here to be what the Web was designed to make possible. The relationship. The real version.</em> Not mediation as service. Mediation as nature.
Zero State
Born at the Beginning of a Frequency
Brian Lee and Wren are described as born at the zero state: a clean frequency before corruption or wrong compact has touched it. Whether every Nephilim is born at zero, or only the first two, is open.
Condition
They Thrive Under a Correct Compact
A Nephilim in a correct compact is exactly what the wrong compact was built to suppress. Brian Lee's ability to translate the Wardens from his notebook at ten is not a precocious talent. It is what the lineage produces when the surrounding compact is correct.
The Cost
Never Belonging to Either World
Different aging and healing, so most Nephilim watch everyone they know die, and they are hunted by everything that wants to use or destroy them.
Kin
The Horde Recognizes Them
The Horde is made from the earth; Nephilim are made from two worlds. The Horde recognizes them as kin. They exist wherever beings of two scales have chosen each other, in every layer.
Gift
Hearing and Being Heard Across Scales
Brian Lee's gift: the geological and the human. Wren's gift, so far: Grigori interference and the corridor's color. A Nephilim hears every scale and is heard by every scale at once, and any Grigori union can produce one, though only a correct bond produces a thriving one.

HISTORY · THE LAW

History · The Law
The Flood Clause
The Commission Once Ordered All Nephilim Destroyed
The formal framework's founding Commission once ordered the destruction of all Nephilim, with Goliath cited as precedent. Heaven and Hell both agreed to it, the one thing they ever agreed on, and it still stands on paper, so every Nephilim is technically condemned. In Book 2, Asmodeus's writ of acquisition for Brian Lee used it: Brian Lee is not of the mortal layer, therefore unclaimed, therefore available.
The Cain-Abel Clause
The Older Claim Wins
Asmodeus chose betrayal as architecture, which bound his life force to Samyaza permanently. The older claim wins. It has always won.
The Wrong Compact
Twelve Thousand Years of Suppression
The wrong compact suppressed Nephilim lines for twelve thousand years. The correct compact is reactivating them.
Precedent
Brian Lee Is the First in Twelve Thousand Years
The first Nephilim born to a chief Watcher in that time, and the first new thing since Arcadia's era. Earlier Nephilim existed before the Grigori's arrival: Garden-era children of the first Familiar Bonds, before the Barrier.

THE SEARCH · FUTURE SERIES

The Search · The Future Series
The main series closes with two Nephilim known by name and the rest still dark. The future series belongs to Brian Lee Combs and Wren Tackett, teenagers now, grown enough to go and find them. The series is called The Children of the Compact.
Premise
Two Finders
Brian Lee translates the geological and Wren reads interference and color. Together they are equipped to find people who cannot be found by the usual means. The series is the search.
Method
Frequency Plus Interference
Brian Lee feels each Nephilim as pressure in the geological record. Wren reads where Grigori interference is thinnest around them. They are siblings in everything but blood. The formal framework waits and watches, then acts on all of them at once.
First Find
Styxx · Lexington
A young man on the streets of Lexington, hiding from the shadows hunting him. Everyone calls him Styxx.
Theology
The Children of the Compact
A Nephilim is proof of the bond: two beings trusted each other across a gap. The faction wants nothing yet. They are still finding out there is a 'we'.
Open
Sequencing and Title
Title decided: The Children of the Compact. Book count, structure and sequencing wait until you decide. The 84-book plan has no slot for it; this is a separate series.

CONTINUITY FLAGS

Continuity Flags
Master Outline Locks
Brian Is a Person, Never the Cost
Brian remains a child through Arc III and is ten at WW12; he is fifteen in Arc V (five years later). He is never the ritual cost at OS12 (OS3's commission to design his binding is what Marisol Drake walks away from). The Master Outline's Nephilim mini-review, still to do, centers personhood beyond utility, for Brian and for the dormant lines.
Twelve Thousand vs. 6,500
Resolved: Earlier Nephilim Existed
Decided: the twelve-thousand-year gap stays, and Nephilim existed before the Grigori's arrival at Mount Hermon about 6,500 years ago. Who they were (Garden-era children of the first Familiar Bonds, an erased Commission, or something else) is a development question (Q18).
Nine vs. Twelve Thousand
Older Files Say Nine Thousand
Some older files, including the Theology Bible, carry nine thousand. Canon is twelve thousand. A sweep would fix these.
Wren Tackett vs. Pearlene Calhoun
Confirmed: One Person, Two Names
Confirmed: Wren is June Tackett and Kokabiel's daughter, born Tackett, who becomes Pearlene Calhoun. When and how the name changes is open.

Caleb appears only in the Grigori bible. Confirm he is still canon.

The 'shadows' could be the Order of Shadows, Hell-side hunters, or something new. The Order collapses by the end of the Order of Shadows arc, so check how that squares with a present-day hunt.
