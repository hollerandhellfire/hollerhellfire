The Series

Five novels · Pikeville, Kentucky

Appalachian gothic paranormal romance. Six generations of Hollow Witches. Twelve chief Watchers. One corridor that was older than Heaven.

Book One

Fallin' For A Fallen

Raelee "Dixie" Calhoun & Azazel

At twenty-three years old, Raelee Calhoun is the Anchor of the south Pike County Thinning. At the end of a dirt road by a creek, she finds a man who has been sitting on a flat gray rock for ten thousand years and does not want to be saved. She does not try to save him. She comes back the next morning. And the next. And the next.

Book Two

The Devil's in the Details

Della Combs & Samyaza

Della Combs has been running her grandmother's herbal practice for twelve years and raising her seven-year-old son Eli alone. There is a shape at the edge of her east wood. There has been for three weeks. On the twenty-third morning she sets down her basket and walks to the tree line and finds out that she has never once been alone — and that the senior chief Watcher who has been watching her since before she was born is also Eli's father.

Book Three

Strike Me Down

Carla June Maynard & Baraqiel

The Hollow Diner. Table four. Eight months of a man who came in every Tuesday and never ordered. Carla June Maynard has been the corridor's warm room for twelve years. The chief Watcher of lightning has been her one difficult customer. The notebook is a problem. The lightning is a different problem. The south fence is where it ends.

Book Four

All Hell Breaks Loose

Mercy Tackett & Armaros

Three years alone holding the south border. The Tackett line of the alone. The carrier she was raised to be. Then the chief Watcher Armaros comes to assess her — and stays for the archive. Asmodeus moves. The corridor opens. Mercy closes the line of the alone, opens the line of the open border, and the older mechanism's settlement begins in Pike County.

Book Five

Written in the Stars

June Tackett & Kokabiel

June Tackett is twenty-five, an assistant librarian, and the next carrier of the line. She lives in a small cottage on the south ridge with the chief Watcher of stars and a stack of letters her partner has been writing her since August. She is two months pregnant. Kokabiel has been watching a small star for seventy years. The star is about to do what it has been preparing to do. The framework that has been governing Heaven for ten thousand years is going to have an opinion about it.
