THE ONE LAW

The whole thing is about a relationship built on trust and belief.
The Governing Theme
One Law Under Everything
Every arc, every faction, every bond in the H&H Universe is a variation on a single question: can two beings trust and believe each other across the distance between them? Mortal and Watcher. Daughter and mother. Surface and deep. The condemned and the merciful. The cosmology is elaborate, but it all answers to this. When a thread seems to wander, it is being measured against this law.
What This Document Is
The Master Reference
This is the spine — the condensed, authoritative canon that every other bible expands. The Theology Bible holds the cosmology in full; the faction bibles hold each people; the arc bibles hold the books; the Timeline holds the order of events. This document holds the through-line. Where any file disagrees with this one on a point of core canon, this one is the reference and the other should be corrected to match.
The Shape of the Story
A Correction, Not a War
The series is not good versus evil. It is the long correction of institutions that mistook the authority they inherited for authority they hold — Heaven's formal framework and Hell's faction alike — back toward the older, truer order they both sit beneath: the daughters' practice, the Older Mechanism, and the Arcana that precede them all.
Dedication — For every woman who held a door before she knew what the door was for.

The Barrier
Sano's Barrier and the Corridors
Sano created the Barrier around the Mortal Layer; it later trapped intersecting portions of Heaven and Hell, allowing no passage on any level and producing the static interference. About 6,500 years ago the Grigori arrived from Mount Hermon; contact with the Barrier rewired them into mortal forms and created the Corridors. The Barrier is destroyed at OS12. The Veil is a separate layer and shatters at DA5.
Everything the formal framework claimed authority over was always older than the framework's authority — and Hell, its opposite number, is no higher. Both sit at the bottom, on one plane, opposing each other, downstream of everything above.

THE FIGURES

The Twins & The Sun
The Darkness
Nepisano
Croatian: "Unwritten." Sano's Gemini twin and beloved brother; cool, rational, and not originally malicious. King of Hell who left his kingdom to the Princes and went into the deepest Unwritten — further than Abaddon's Abyss. Hecate's uncle. The Darkness is not corruption: Andras found a real wound and gave it a false meaning (the First Lie — love is not real, everyone forgets you). The corruption was removed long ago; the wound remained. The brother who thinks he was forgotten — and whose healing is relational, not purification into Light.
The Sun · Arcana XIX
Sano
Croatian: "Enlightened." Nepisano's twin. Married to Gaia (the Empress). Father of Hecate (the Moon). His Bang made the specific solar-system Creation of the H&H cosmology, not all existence. He did not intentionally reject Nepisano, but he failed to notice how alone his brother had become. When Hecate is taken (DA5) and the Founding Factions fail to find her (DA6), his grief becomes solar force.
Cain, Abaddon & Arcadia
First Regent of Hell
Lucifer = Cain
The first murderer. Killed Abel (favored by Gaia and Hecate) out of Pride; killed Arcadia when Satan's corruption drove him to it, during her intended Familiar Bond with Leviathan. The Dzin pursued and marked him. Mark of Three Slashes on his shoulder. Survived the flood, old age, millennia in hiding, and Hell's full assault with his humanity mostly and his pride entirely intact. Nepisano found him, named him Lucifer, made him First Regent. Lucifer is Cain / Pride and is a separate being from Satan. At OS12 his willing sacrifice reconciles his blood debt and returns his soul to zero state — reconciliation is not redemption. He faces Nepisano in SC11.
The Abyss
Abaddon
The oldest soul in Hell — and the only one Hell has never corrupted, because he is there willingly, through the deal, and the cold has no claim on a soul that chose to come. In life a dragon slayer; he invoked Strength, killed Satan's dragon body, and died. His mortal magic alone could never have carved the Abyss or outlasted its monsters. The deal with Sano lends him the power his magic could not manage on its own. He answers to no one. Arcadia's husband. The Abyss sits on the only road out of the deepest Unwritten, and it is distinct from Hell — so when Nepisano finally returns, he stops there, finds Abaddon, and says only: "Coming…?" (Exact placement is pending the Abyss / Abaddon audit.) Abaddon enters the story directly in DA11.
The First Weavers
Arcadia, Celeste & Lyrica
Arcadia was Abaddon's wife — the first Omni Witch, who chose Leviathan for her Familiar Bond, wrote the first word — HERE — above his heart, and was killed by Cain under the push of Satan's corruption. Her twin daughters by Abaddon — Celeste (named for the Star Arcana) and Lyrica (the unsung music of creation) — were adopted by Gaia and became the first Weavers. Their protection by Sano IS the Web compact.
The Prince of Wrath
The Red Dragon
Satan
A red firedrake — Leviathan's younger brother (Leviathan is the older brother and an autonomous person, not a mechanism). Andras repeated against him the corruption tactic he used on Nepisano, and that corruption drove Cain to Arcadia's murder. Abaddon killed his dragon body, and the frequency of that grief-battle reached Sano, which is where the deal was made. His Death Spell was contained by Leviathan, the Wardens, and the Horde. He is Wrath. At OS10 the locks on his soul fail and he walks out of Hell; at OS12 the Davos corruption is purified from his soul and he returns to zero state — his exact Seventh Cycle reveal is author-placed.

THE FACTIONS

Each people has a full bible of its own; this is the place each holds in the order.
The Arcana
22 Primordials · frequency, not authority
The foundational frequencies of the universe, prior to every system built to describe them. They operate by recognition, not intervention. The Sun (Sano) and the Moon (Hecate) belong here.
The Grigori
200 Watchers · commissioned through the Lovers
Commissioned to accompany the daughters' practice — not enforce it. The formal framework reframed relationship as surveillance; the series is their return to true function.
The Nephilim
The in-between · working faction · about two dozen
Neither human nor Watcher: they hear every scale and are heard by every scale at once. Brian Lee Combs, Wren Tackett and Styxx are confirmed. The Flood Clause, never repealed, condemns them all. Their own later series: The Children of the Compact.
Heaven's Formal Framework
The institutional claim · enforcement
The institution that mistook the authority it inherited for authority it holds. Downstream of the Web it tried to govern. Sits at the bottom plane, opposing Hell.
Hell's Faction
The abandoned kingdom · seven Princes
A society made from a wound, not Darkness itself: Nepisano's wounded creator-frequency produced it, with Heaven as its parallel balance. A Five-Prince council governs after Lucifer. The Wrong Compact fed the locks on Satan's soul. Hell survives as Hell without becoming a dumping ground (SC12).
Mortal Practitioners
The daughters · the Hollow Witches
The living practice, 30,000 years old, that the compact protects. The Web's expression at the human scale — no longer junior to anyone after the Web Compact.
The Horde
The geological family · the earth awake
Autonomous geological peoples, not an army: Leviathan is the relational center, not king, and Samyaza translates rather than commands. The Mortal Layer's body. Their emergence is disorientation, not invasion.
The Darkness
Nepisano · the Unwritten
Not absence but presence — the primal force that has worked since before the practice had language. One technique, the lie, and infinite patience.
The Bone Mother
Keeper of the dead · origin of Hell
Master Gatekeeper, Archive, and Time — and the literal mother of Sano and Nepisano (the pre-Creation history is reserved). She is not a moral judge: her Gate is structurally closed while a Cycle is unresolved. She releases souls, not memory. The Archive keeps the truthful record permanently.

FOUNDATIONAL LAWS

Law One
The Frequency Principle
The body's frequency rises with emotional intensity. Stronger emotion means stronger frequency means stronger work. Fear amplifies dark arcana; love sustains the daughters' practice. Every death releases a frequency — Hecate reads it to open the passage, down the Corridor, to the Bone Mother's realm.
Law Two
The Moon Phases
Hecate's vigil is the mortal layer's primary protection. Full Moon: her attention at peak — the worst night for demons. New Moon: her attention most inward — peak demonic activity.
Law Three
The Compact, the Bond & the Web
The Older Mechanism — bond law and compact law — predates the formal framework and every Prince's negotiation. Its record cannot be falsified; the daughters' practice carries it in living form, and the geological record (the Horde) keeps it where it cannot be taken. The compact is trust made structural.
The Corruption
Vine, the Church & the Dark Ages
Vine — a Duke, formerly a mortal witch-hunter who discovered the death-frequency principle — brought Lucifer the idea of the Church: harvest fear-death frequency from witch persecutions and suppress the daughters' lateral connections. In 1414 Vine's architecture was also used to feed the containment locks holding Satan's soul, and the Order of the Shadows became the institutional machinery around that burden. The Dark Ages were the direct consequence.

KEY EVENTS

The First Deception
The First Lie
Andras found a real wound in Nepisano and assigned it a false meaning, and under that corruption Nepisano attacked Gaia. Hecate's Light destroyed the corruption but not the wound or the meaning he had attached to it. He fled into the deep, and his wounded creator-frequency produced Hell. The real wound and the false conclusion are separate: none of it proves love was false. (Earlier text in which the Darkness lured Hecate to a boundary is superseded by the Master Outline's origin chain.)
The Deal
Abaddon & Sano at the Grave
When Abaddon slew the Red Dragon at Arcadia's grave, the frequency of that grief-battle reached Sano. The deal: Sano protects Abaddon's twin daughters and requires Abaddon to bring Nepisano back whole — but not before then. That protection founded the Web compact.
The Locks on Satan
Satan, Contained Then Freed
About twelve thousand years ago: Leviathan, the Wardens, and the Horde contained Satan's Death Spell with their bodies. 1414: Vine's architecture began feeding the locks on Satan's soul with stolen Practitioner frequency. OS10: the locks fail for good and Satan walks out of Hell; OS11: he takes over the Catholic Church; OS12: his corruption is purified and he returns to zero state.
The Turn · OS12 → DA1
Lucifer's Sacrifice & the Gap
In Rome at OS12, Lucifer becomes a willing binding agent knowing the cost — to make right his two murders. The Barrier is destroyed and Mreza awakens; Lucifer and Satan both leave Hell for the Arcana Layer, in different places. The Church of Hell falls and the Mortal Layer broadcasts again (DA1). Hecate is not taken at DA1. She is taken at DA5 (The Gap), and the Veil shatters completely. Nepisano is explicitly not her abductor; the Davos are the principal ancient mystery and active threat. Brian is never the ritual cost.

THE ARCS

The compact opens on the Fire Solstice — the longest day, the sun at its peak. The Fire Solstice remains in the Older Mechanism's calendar; there is no "seven-year" governing concept. The final cosmic Restoration belongs to SC12, the last book of the Seventh Cycle.
