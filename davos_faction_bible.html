ORIGIN

Every other faction began clean and could fall. The Davos were wrong from the first instant of awareness — corruption already in them when the spheres separated.
Genesis · The Original Corrupted
Before Territory
The Bang · Already Wrong
At the Bang — when Sano pulled the space-dust together into ten spheres inside a huge eleventh — the assembled factions came to awareness and demanded answers. Among the voices were the Davos: the 12 Bars — the original corrupted. Unlike every other faction, the Davos did not begin clean and fall; they were wrong from the first instant of awareness. The warden-monsters of the Abyss, present from the very beginning, corruption already in them when the spheres separated.
What They Are
Continuity That Survived Incorrectly
The Davos are the warden-monsters that hold the Abyss. Their frequencies are wrong and distorted: instead of ceasing to exist as a broken frequency should, they adapted to anti-relational existence pressure — continuity that survived incorrectly. This is the deepest horror in the cosmology: not a thing that was made evil, but a thing that should not have persisted at all and persisted anyway, in the only shape persistence-without-relationship can take. They are the antithesis of the dragons (creation taken form); the Davos are corruption taken form. (The Abyss itself — the layer of leftover creation they ward — is its own faction; see the Abyss faction bible.)
Territory
Pluto · As Far From the Light As Possible
The Davos could not stand Sano and his Light, but they responded to Nepisano and his cool logic. To be as far from Sano as possible, they took Pluto — the outermost, small, dark, frozen world. The seed of life did not take there. And they felt they had been tricked — a resentment that is part of the shape of their corruption. The frozen Plutonian dark is the natural home of continuity that refused both ceasing and relationship.

THE FIRST CORRUPTION

One Davos, one lie, and creation has been trying to heal from it ever since. The lie is always the same shape: that love is not real.
The First Corruption · Andras and the Lie
The Leader
Andras · The Most Ruthless
The Davos hierarchy is pure power — rank is held by ruthlessness alone — and at the top is Andras, the most ruthless of all. He is not a king in any lawful sense; he is simply the worst, and among the Davos the worst leads. Andras is the source of the single most consequential act in the entire history of Sano’s Creation.
The First Lie
The First Lie Told to Nepisano · The Origin of Creation’s Corruption
It was Andras who whispered the first lie told to Nepisano, the Darkness, Sano’s twin brother: ‘They don’t love you… love is not real…’ This is the ORIGIN of the Corruption in Sano’s Creation. Every wound in this Creation traces back to this whisper: the slow madness that took Nepisano, the attack on Gaia, the doorway frequency, Heaven and Hell, the seven cycles. One Davos, one lie, and creation has been trying to heal from it ever since. The lie is always the same shape — that love is not real, that connection is not real, that there is nothing worth trusting. It is not the first lie in the cosmos. A world existed before Sano’s Creation, and a betrayal of the Hearth’s bond came earlier there; Andras had no part in it (see the Pre-Creation Bible).
The Repeated Tactic
The Same Lie, a New Ear
The Davos use the same tactic wherever it works, only changing the ear they whisper into. The lie that took Nepisano took Satan the same way (‘They don’t love you. How could anyone love a hothead like you?’), and it is the tactic used on the corrupted again and again, and the one Andras uses at scale in SC5: true wounds turned into false conclusions. There are exactly twelve Davos. They do not multiply across Cycles and they carry no memory from one Cycle to the next; the lie is always the same shape, finding a new ear. The Davos sow the inversion of the first true thing, the relationship built on trust and belief.
Why It Works — and Fails
The Counter to the Lie
The lie works on hope, fear, and feeling — things that can be corrupted. But it cannot reach a chosen action. The cosmology’s deepest counter: hope is a feeling and can be corrupted; trust and belief are chosen verbs and cannot. A relationship built on trust that is test-proven, with belief as its foundation and acceptance as its resonance, is the specific thing the Davos’s lie cannot lie away. Accumulated love is the exact counter to accumulated corruption — which is why the Restoration (SC12) is built bond by bond, each one a choosing the Davos cannot reach.

THE BARS

The Bars · The Cell of the Threshold
The Cell
The Bars in the Threefold Order
The Bone Mother, as Master Gatekeeper, oversees the three twelves of the threshold system: the Keys (Voljeti), the Gates (Nasilin), and the Bars (Davos) — the lock, the door, and the cell. The Davos are the BARS: the warden-wall, what the whole threshold-system is built in part to keep shut. The Voljeti’s Keys set the lawful terms; the Nasilin’s Gates are the doors; the Davos’s Bars are what holds the cell. The Bone Mother holds even the Bars — the corrupted are within the order, contained by it, not outside it.
Keepers of the Damned
What the Bars Hold
As the Bars, the Davos are bound to the cell — continuity that survived incorrectly, kept where it can be kept. They are at once the corrupted AND the containment for the worst of anti-relational existence: the cell-wall made of the very thing it walls against. This is the grim logic of the threshold — the only thing that can hold the wrongness is more of the same wrongness, ranked and bound under the Bone Mother’s threshold-order at the frozen edge of the spheres. What they ward is the Abyss: the layer of leftover creation and stranded souls set beneath the Unwritten (its own faction bible).
Andras and the Abyss
Wardens of the Abyss
The Davos are the warden-monsters of the Abyss — the named and ranked beings set against its contents, holding the layer from Pluto’s side while Abaddon keeps his vigil at the edge of creation — the Abyss is bounded and distinct from Hell. Andras and his eleven are the bottom of the cosmology’s wrongness — not a force or a pressure but persons, corrupted persons, who chose and choose the lie. They are not the Abyss’s leftover creatures; they are the Bars that keep those creatures kept.
The Distinction
Davos vs the Hell Faction
The Davos must not be confused with Hell. Hell is downstream — a realm born from Nepisano’s wounded mind, populated by corrupted souls. The Davos are upstream and original — primordial corrupted, present from the Bang, the source of the lie that made Hell possible. The Princes of Hell were corrupted later; the Davos were never anything else. Andras whispering to Nepisano is the cause; Hell is one of the effects.

STANDING CANON

Standing Canon · Fixed & Reserved
Locked Canon
What Is Fixed
The Davos are twelve. The 12 Bars — the original corrupted, present from the Bang, the warden-monsters of the Abyss. Territory: Pluto (small, dark, frozen; the seed did not take; they felt tricked). Their leader is ANDRAS, the most ruthless (Davos hierarchy is pure power). Andras whispered the first lie told to Nepisano — the origin of the Corruption in Sano’s Creation — and the same tactic is used on the corrupted (Satan, and others) in every cycle. It is not the first lie in the cosmos: the old world’s frame of Igren came earlier, and Andras had no part in it. They are the Bars (the cell) in the threshold system’s lock-door-cell: Keys / Gates / Bars = Voljeti / Nasilin / Davos. They are continuity that survived incorrectly — the antithesis of the dragons (creation taken form). The Abyss they ward is a distinct faction/layer, not the Davos themselves. These points are fixed by the cosmogony and The Codex.
Open / Reserved
Held for Later
All twelve are now named: I Andras, II Illic, III Karas, IV Kanta, V Sol, VI Divitiae, VII Amator, VIII Regnum, IX Rego, X Victoria, XI Umbra, XII Forma (several Latin — the Davos’s tongue and the language of corruption). Andras is the named leader and the source of the first lie told to Nepisano; the individual domains and rankings of the other eleven are reserved for development. Any Davos who steps onto the page as a character beyond Andras is reserved for development. (Note: the Dukes and Princes of Hell are a separate, downstream faction — see the Hell faction bible — not to be conflated with the primordial Davos.)
Master Outline
Seventh Cycle Placement (Master Outline)
Identity: twelve Bars; the original corrupted; Andras leads. There are exactly twelve; they do not multiply across Cycles and carry no cross-Cycle memory. Placement: primary in SC2, SC5, SC10, SC11, and SC12. They win when distinctions collapse and true facts are turned into isolating conclusions. In the Divine Arc they are the principal ancient mystery and active threat, exposed at DA12. Final state: contained corruption, not governing corruption — the lie loses structural authority, and the Davos are not erased.
