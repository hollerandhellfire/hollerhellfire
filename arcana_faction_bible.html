The 22 Arcana
The Primordial Forces \u00b7 The Master Builder\u2019s Structural Set
Twenty-two foundational frequencies that existed before creation organized itself \u2014 the forces the Tarot and the Hebrew alphabet describe, the guardians of the Highways of the Tree of Life, operating not by command but by the recognition of a frequency correctly chosen.
Origin
They are not gods. They are not beings who chose form for themselves. They are the 22 primordial forces that existed before creation organized itself \u2014 and took form because form was needed.
Genesis \u00b7 The Foundational Frequencies
Before Territory
The Bang · Forces Already Present
At the Bang — when Sano pulled the space-dust together so hard it broke into ten spheres inside a huge eleventh — the assembled primordial factions came to awareness and demanded answers. Among the first voices were the Arcana: 22 primordial forces. They are not gods, not angels, not practitioners writ large. They are the foundational frequencies of the universe — the specific forces that exist prior to every system built to describe them. The H&H universe did not summon them. It arrived in their presence.
The Structural Set
The Master Builder’s Frequencies
The 22 Arcana are the Master Builder’s structural set — the complete catalogue of foundational forces creation needed in order to organize itself: something to be the Law, something to be the Bond, something to be the Turn, something to be the Sacrifice. They are described by the 22 Major Arcana of the Tarot and the 22 letters of the Hebrew alphabet. Tarot cards describe them because human pattern recognition found them. The cards are the description; the Primordials are what the description points at.
Territory
Venus · Never Contained
When territories were apportioned, the Arcana took Venus for their home — but they could never be contained. Their nature is to operate as forces across all of creation, not to be bounded to a single sphere. They dwell, like all the founding factions, on the Arcana Layer — the layer named for them because they are its foundational structural forces, though it is the common plane of all primordial existence.
The Balance
22 Forces, 22 Beasts
The Arcana do not stand alone. They are matched by the 22 Sjen — the shadow-aligned primordial beasts who took Mars. 22 forces of light-order and 22 beasts of shadow-order, mirrored. The Sjen are not corrupt; they are the necessary dark that makes the light meaningful, the same way uncorrupted Nepisano balanced Sano. Light-order and shadow-order, the great 22-and-22 symmetry of the founding roster.
The 22

The complete set of foundational forces \u2014 each with its primordial name, the Tarot card and Hebrew letter that describe it, its assigned Highway on the Tree of Life, and its role in the saga. Gold \u2014 central to the saga\u2019s resolution. Struck \u2014 the Moon, displaced in the Divine Arc.

The one who named things into existence before the Older Mechanism had a framework to receive them. Older than every archive. The force of the spoken word that makes real — the meta-frequency principle at the primordial scale.

Nature \u00b7 How the Forces Operate
How They Act
Frequency and Recognition, Not Intervention
Most Arcana do not intervene in the mortal layer. They operate through frequency: the specific resonance in a correct bond that the Lovers recognizes; the specific quality of a warm room that Strength noticed. When the Strength Arcana notices the Calhoun line for seven generations, it is not the card choosing the line — it is the card recognizing the frequency that has been running in the line. The practitioner chose the frequency; the Arcana recognized the match.
Not Chosen — Matched
The Practitioners Who Carry Arcana Frequencies
The practitioners who carry Arcana frequencies are not chosen by the cards. They are running at a card’s frequency because of who they are and what they chose. The card noticed. That is the entire relationship: the practitioner chose the frequency; the Arcana recognized it. Nothing is imposed downward — which is exactly how the Arcana differ from the corruption, whose method is imposition.
When They Step In
Direct Engagement Means Something Broke
When an Arcana steps into direct engagement with the mortal layer, it is because something has broken that was never supposed to break. The Magician’s attention on an arc is itself the first sign of a rupture. The Arcana acting directly is an alarm, not a routine — the foundational forces do not reach down into mortal affairs unless the structure itself is threatened.
Older Than the Law
The Arcana and the Older Mechanism
The Arcana are older than the Older Mechanism. The Older Mechanism is what the Arcana Layer produced when it needed something to govern the space between itself and the mortal world — the bond law and compact law, built by the 12 Voljeti (the Keys) and encoded under the Arcana Layer’s authority. The Arcana are the forces; the Older Mechanism is the law those forces required. The forces came first.
The 22 Highways
The Highways \u00b7 The Guarded Ascent
The Map of Ascent
The 22 Pathways of the Tree of Life
On the Kabbalistic Tree of Life — the Mortal Layer’s most accurate map of both the cosmological stack and the eleven levels of awareness — the 22 pathways connecting the spheres are the Highways. They are numbered as they are in the original Tree, and they correspond to the 22 Arcana and the 22 Hebrew letters. The Highways are the relational connections between frequency states: the roads a soul travels on its ascent.
The Guardians
Held by the Arcana and the Sjen Together
Each Highway is guarded by two beings — one Arcana and its single Sjen counterpart, light-order force and shadow-order beast paired across the path. The one exception is Highway 8 (Cheth), guarded by four: the Lovers Arcana — themselves the pair Svaler and Ljubanica — and their Sjen counterparts, the paired beasts Gasiq and Huzabike. The Highway of the Bond is the only two-and-two crossing, because the Lovers and their shadow are each already a pair (the Bond takes two on both sides). The lower the number on the Highway, the stronger the frequency of the ones guarding it. The most powerful Arcana and Sjen hold the lowest-numbered Highways; the higher-numbered paths are held by weaker guardians. This is the structural reason ascent grows harder, not easier, the deeper a soul reaches.
The Count
Two Guardians Per Highway
Each Highway is guarded by two beings — one Arcana and its single Sjen counterpart, the light-order force and the shadow-order beast that hold the same path. The one exception is Highway 8 (Cheth), guarded by four: the Lovers Arcana (the pair Svaler and Ljubanica) and their Sjen counterparts (the paired beasts Gasiq and Huzabike). The Highway of the Bond is the only two-and-two crossing, because the Lovers and their shadow are each already a pair — the Bond takes two on both sides of the balance. (This is distinct from the soul’s awareness-journey, where a climbing soul encounters multiple primordial beings as the challenges of each level’s frequency — see The Codex. The Highways are the guarded pathways; the levels are the ascent.)

Standing Canon
Standing Canon \u00b7 Fixed & Reserved
Locked Canon
What Is Fixed
The Arcana are twenty-two. Primordial forces — the foundational frequencies of the universe, prior to every system that describes them. Described by the 22 Major Arcana and the 22 Hebrew letters. Territory: Venus (never contained); they dwell on the Arcana Layer with all the factions. Matched by the 22 Sjen. They guard the 22 Highways of the Tree of Life alongside the Sjen — two per Highway (one Arcana, one Sjen), except Highway 8 (Cheth), held by four (the Lovers pair and their Sjen counterparts). Lower number = stronger guardian. They operate by frequency and recognition, not intervention. Hecate is the Moon Arcana in person. These points are fixed by the cosmogony and The Codex.
Open / Reserved
Held for Later
The per-Arcana character roles rendered here (drawn from the Divine Arc bible — the Magician as the First Word, the Hermit as the Witness, and so on) are the working organizing scaffold for how each force operates in the saga. Individual Arcana who step onto the page as active characters, and the specific Sjen-pairings on each numbered Highway, are reserved for development.
