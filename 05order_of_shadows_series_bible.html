PREMISE

What This Arc Is
Placement: five years after WW12. Brian is fifteen.
The Order was built around a lie — but the lie was performing a necessary function.

A necessary purpose does not make an abusive method righteous. If a burden must be carried, the people carrying it must be allowed to choose.
The Arc’s Central Mechanism
Five Defections. Failing Locks. One Chosen Convergence.
The arc opens with four defections, each precisely different from the others, and adds a fifth at OS8:

Cassius gives the network the enforcement protocol architecture and the target list.

Vania gives the network the seal fragment and 40 years of Vatican sub-level observation.

Marisol gives the network the complete 600-year archive and the full seal schematic.

Anselm gives the network the founding generation's testimony — what the document cannot carry.

Samsapeel (OS8) turns defection itself into infrastructure as the Web moves toward Rome.

Each defector arrived with exactly what the next step required. This is not coincidence. This is the Older Mechanism’s bond law operating at its full expression: the correct action produces the conditions for the next correct action.
The Order’s True Architecture
The Machinery Around the Locks
In 1414 Vine’s architecture was used to feed stolen Practitioner frequency into the containment locks holding Satan’s soul, and the Order of the Shadows became the institutional machinery around that burden. The seven founding brothers took the oath believing they were serving Hell's institutional interests.\n\nThe lock holds a real danger — the Death Spell Satan carried was contained about twelve thousand years ago by the Leviathan, the Wardens and the Horde. The Order’s method was abusive; the function it performed was necessary. That is the arc’s tension.\n\nThe Order's collapse was built into its founding logic. OS10 is where the Web finally rejects suffering-as-maintenance and the locks fail for good.
Series Position
Five Years After the Web · Before the Divine Arc
The OS arc operates on the structural foundation the Web of the Weavers arc established:

The 443-person lateral network (WW1) means no defector, and no practitioner, carries this alone.

The Omni Witch function (WW12) and Edna Mae’s witness make Anselm’s testimony something the network can hold.

And what the OS arc establishes seeds the Divine Arc: the Barrier is destroyed at OS12, the Mortal Layer broadcasts again, and Mreza awakens.
