The 22 Sjen
The Changeling Beasts \u00b7 The Shadow-Order \u00b7 The Necessary Dark
Twenty-two shape-changing primordial beasts who took Mars and began the hunt \u2014 the shadow-order counterpart to the 22 Arcana, the rightful dark that makes the light meaningful, guarding the Highways of the Tree of Life alongside the forces they mirror. Not corrupt. Necessary.
Origin
22 forces of light-order, 22 beasts of shadow-order, matched. The Sjen are not the enemy of the light. They are the dark that gives the light its meaning.
Genesis \u00b7 The Shadow-Order Beasts
Before Territory
The Bang · The Beasts Came Forward
At the Bang — when Sano pulled the space-dust together into ten spheres inside a huge eleventh — the assembled factions came to awareness and demanded answers. Among the voices were the Sjen: 22 changeling beasts (also recorded as the Sjena). They are the shadow BEASTS of the founding roster — primordial, shape-changing, of the dark. Where the Arcana are the 22 forces of light-order, the Sjen are the 22 beasts of shadow-order.
The Symmetry
22 Forces, 22 Beasts
The Sjen are the balancing counterpart to the 22 Arcana — the great 22-and-22 symmetry at the heart of the founding roster. For every Arcana force of light-order, a Sjen beast of shadow-order. This is not light versus dark in the corruption’s sense. It is the necessary pairing of the two halves of one balance: the structural force and its shadow, mirrored twenty-two and twenty-two, the same balance that the brothers Sano and Nepisano embody at the highest scale.
Territory
Mars · And the Hunt Began
When territories were apportioned, the Sjen took Mars — and the hunt began. The hunt is the Sjen’s defining motion: the shadow-order in pursuit, the predator-frequency that gives the light-order something to be distinct against. Mars, the red sphere, the hunter’s world. Like all the founding factions they dwell on the Arcana Layer, each within their apportioned territory.
The Necessary Dark
The Darkness was never evil. The Sjen are the proof at the scale of a faction: 22 beasts of the dark who carry no corruption.
The Necessary Dark \u00b7 Shadow Without Corruption
Not Corrupt
The Sjen Are Not Evil
The single most important fact about the Sjen: they are not corrupt. They are shadow-aligned, predatory, of the dark — but the dark is not the enemy in the H&H universe. The Sjen are the necessary dark that makes the light meaningful, in exactly the same way uncorrupted Nepisano (Darkness) balanced Sano (Light). The corruption is a separate thing — Andras’s lie, the Davos’s anti-relational existence pressure. The Sjen are not that. They are rightful shadow.
The Balance Principle
Why the Dark Is Necessary
Light means nothing without a dark to be distinct against. The receptive requires the active; the rest requires the labor; the hunted gives meaning to the held line. The Sjen are the structural shadow the whole architecture needs to be a balance rather than a single undifferentiated glare. Remove them and the light has nothing to define it. This is the cosmology’s deepest correction to human myth: the dark beasts are not an evil to be slain — they are half of the balance, and the balance is the point.
Changelings
Shape Made Unfixed
The Sjen are changeling beasts — their nature is shape that does not stay fixed. Where the Dzin are force that chose to hold one shape and the Arcana are forces that take form because form is needed, the Sjen are the primordial shadow that shifts: the predator that is never quite the same thing twice, the dark that changes as it moves. Their unfixedness is the shadow-order answer to the Arcana’s structural constancy.
The Counterpart to Nepisano
The Dark That Was Always Good
The Sjen are the faction-scale expression of the truth the whole saga turns on: the Darkness was never evil. Nepisano, the Darkness, was the rightful balance to the Light until a lie corrupted him — and even then the corruption was the foreign thing, not the dark itself. The Sjen are the proof that shadow-order is a legitimate half of creation: 22 beasts of the dark who carry no corruption, hunting on their red world, exactly as they were meant to.
The Hunt
The Hunt \u00b7 The Shadow-Order in Motion
The Defining Motion
The Hunt as Shadow-Order Function
The hunt began the moment the Sjen took Mars. The hunt is their function in the balance: the shadow-order in active pursuit, the predator-frequency that keeps the light-order honest. In a balanced creation, the hunt is not atrocity — it is the necessary pressure that gives form its edge, the dark motion against which the light’s held line means something.
On the Journey
The Sjen as Challenge
As a soul climbs the eleven levels of awareness, the primordial beings it encounters at each level include the Sjen as the shadow-order challenges — the dark counterpart to the Arcana’s light-order challenges. How a soul receives a Sjen’s frequency — whether it can meet the shadow without being consumed or fleeing — is part of how it advances. The hunt, met correctly, is passed; met wrongly, it overwhelms.
Not the Corruption’s Tool
The Hunt vs Consumption
The Sjen’s hunt must not be confused with the Davos’s consumption. The hunt is predation within the balance; consumption is the corruption’s anti-relational survival. A Sjen hunts as its rightful function in a balanced creation. A Davos consumes others to survive incorrectly. Same surface appearance — a dark thing pursuing — opposite principle. The Sjen belong to the balance; the Davos are the break in it.

The Highways
The Highways \u00b7 Guarded with the Arcana
The Guarded Ascent
Sjen and Arcana on the Highways
On the Kabbalistic Tree of Life — the map of both the cosmological stack and the eleven levels of awareness — the 22 Highways (the pathways between the spheres) are guarded — two beings per Highway: one Arcana and its single Sjen counterpart, light-order force and shadow-order beast paired across the path. The one exception is Highway 8 (Cheth), held by four: the Lovers Arcana (the pair Svaler and Ljubanica) and their Sjen counterparts (the paired beasts Gasiq and Huzabike). The Highway of the Bond is the only two-and-two crossing, because both the force and its shadow are already pairs there.
The Strength Rule
Lower Number, Stronger Guardian
The lower the number on the Highway, the stronger the frequency of the ones guarding it. The most powerful Sjen, like the most powerful Arcana, hold the lowest-numbered Highways; the higher-numbered paths are held by weaker guardians. This is why ascent grows harder, not easier, the deeper a soul reaches — the strongest shadow-beasts stand on the lowest, deepest roads, alongside the strongest forces.
The Pairing
Each Sjen to Its Arcana
Each Sjen is the shadow-order counterpart to a specific Arcana — the beast that hunts the same Highway the force holds, two guardians to a path. Each pair’s Highway assignment is canon (shown on every roster card); the narrative shadow-descriptions are the scaffold for development. What is fixed: 22 Sjen matched to the 22 Arcana, guarding the 22 Highways two-per-path, with Highway 8 (Cheth) the lone four-guardian exception where the Lovers-pair (Svaler and Ljubanica) and the Sjen pair (Gasiq and Huzabike) hold the Bond together.
Standing Canon
Standing Canon \u00b7 Fixed & Reserved
Locked Canon
What Is Fixed
The Sjen are twenty-two. Changeling beasts — the shadow-aligned primordial beasts (also recorded as the Sjena). Territory: Mars — and the hunt began. They are the balancing counterpart to the 22 Arcana (22 forces of light-order, 22 beasts of shadow-order). They are NOT corrupt — they are the necessary dark that makes the light meaningful, the same way uncorrupted Nepisano balanced Sano. They guard the 22 Highways of the Tree of Life alongside the Arcana — two per Highway (one Arcana, one Sjen), except Highway 8 (Cheth), held by four (the Lovers pair Svaler and Ljubanica and the Sjen pair Gasiq and Huzabike). Lower number = stronger guardian. They dwell on the Arcana Layer. These points are fixed by the cosmogony and The Codex.
Open / Reserved
Held for Later
The per-Sjen identities rendered here (each as the shadow-counterpart to an Arcana) are a working organizing scaffold, not yet hard canon — the cosmogony fixes the count (22) and the symmetry (matched to the Arcana) but does not yet name or individuate the beasts. The specific Sjen-to-Arcana Highway pairings, any named Sjen, and any Sjen who step onto the page as characters are reserved for development. Naming note: the master Origin records them as Sjena; The Codex and project canon use Sjen — used here as the primary form.
