PREMISE

The Inciting Event
The seal breaks on a Tuesday. Not the corridor seal — something beneath the corridor. A pressure shift at a depth the anchor bond has never registered before.

Dixie feels it at 3:47 AM. Azazel was already awake. He says I don't know — which in ten thousand years he does not say casually.
The Arc in One Sentence
The Web begins to be correctly constituted — and the oldest thing in the world wakes up.
The Leviathan has been in the deep position since Arcadia's choosing twelve thousand years ago. As the network builds the Web's correct frequency, the seal on the wrong compact deteriorates. What emerges is the oldest living thing in the Appalachian system reaching toward the relationship it was always supposed to have.
Foundation
What This Arc Establishes for Everything That Follows
The Arcana layer's record of the New Compact is part of the Familiar-bond lineage the later arcs draw on. The Lovers Arcana traces the Familiar bond — Arcadia's yes and the Leviathan's yes — to its correct expression here at WMB12.
Primary Arc Antagonist
Asmodeus — Prince of Lust · Hell's Second-Ranked Authority
His sin is Lust but his domain is the specific corruption of bonds — the corruption of the choosing that makes a bond possible. He cannot destroy a bond. He can only corrupt the conditions that make choosing possible. He comes with paperwork: a writ of acquisition for Brian Lee Combs, the most significant leverage asset Hell's council has seen in twelve thousand years. (Asmodeus’s writ is an Arc I, Book 2 event; he is neutralized from Book 2 onward.) The Cain-Abel clause binds him to Samyaza permanently. He is functionally neutralized from Book 2 onward. The older claim wins. It has always won.
The Arc's Deeper Antagonist Structure
Satan — Prince of Wrath · the Former Red Firedrake · The Wrong Compact’s Hidden Burden
About twelve thousand years ago Abaddon killed Satan’s dragon body and died; the Leviathan, the Wardens, and the Horde contained the Death Spell with their bodies. In 1414 Vine’s architecture began feeding stolen Practitioner frequency into the locks on Satan’s soul, and the Order of the Shadows became the machinery around that burden. The recent injury sits on top of an older grievance: humanity never honored what the Horde sacrificed.

The New Compact at WMB12 does not resolve any of this — it accelerates the decay of the locks. The arc keeps Satan, Lucifer, the Davos and the Cycles offstage and unrevealed. The later Order of the Shadows arc takes the consequences.

MYTHOLOGY

Mythology & Mechanisms
Mechanism
The Familiar Bond
The first Familiar bond — Arcadia saying yes to the Leviathan twelve thousand years ago. The Web is built from that choosing. The correct version is relationship, not containment. (The first bond of all was older still: the brothers Sano and Nepisano, at the origin. Arcadia and the Leviathan are the first Familiar bond in the mortal layer — the pattern the whole Web is built from.)
Mechanism
The Leviathan's Origin — Gaia's First Creation
The Leviathan is not a monster that arrived later. He is Gaia's first real creation — made when the child Hecate took a life-seed from her mother's Garden without asking, shaped clay to resemble the primordial beast Agron, and the clay moved, opened its eyes, stood, and smiled. She named him Leviathan. Her best friend. Agron is the First of the twelve Nasilin — the Gates, the primordial beasts who serve only the Bone Mother — a dragon big enough to swallow a planet. The Leviathan was modeled on that first Gate. So the oldest living thing beneath Pike County is, at its root, a child's act of love shaped after the deepest primordial architecture: Gaia's first creation, formed in the image of the Bone Mother's first Gate. (See the Nasilin faction bible and The Codex.)
Mechanism
The First Word
Here. Written by Arcadia in the soil above the Leviathan's heart. Still in the geology. Brian Lee feels it — not me recognizing it. It recognizing me. Arcadia was murdered by Cain — driven by Satan’s corruption — during her intended Familiar Bond with the Leviathan, immediately after she wrote the word. The first word survives because the geological record cannot be taken.
Mechanism
Satan, the Death Spell, and the Locks
The wrong compact was not merely an incorrect version of the Familiar bond’s architecture. It became the machinery that fed the locks on Satan’s soul. About twelve thousand years ago Abaddon invoked Strength, killed Satan’s dragon body, and died. Satan’s Death Spell was then contained by the Leviathan, the Wardens, and the Horde — with their bodies. That is the older sacrifice, and humanity never honored it. In 1414, Vine learned to use the old containment architecture as a conduit for stolen Practitioner frequency, feeding the locks on Satan’s soul; the Order of the Shadows became the institutional machinery around that burden. The recent injury is layered on the older grievance: the Horde had already paid, and was never thanked. The arc’s locks (Master Outline): Lucifer does not appear on-page as an arc participant; consequences of containment destabilization stay offstage until later. The Davos, the Cycles, Sano’s Barrier, Mreza’s identity, the full Arcadia/Abaddon family truth, and the full Satan history stay unrevealed. WMB12 accelerates the decay of the locks rather than resolving the later Order crisis (OS10).
Mechanism
The Wrong Compact
Fear-and-control architecture that suppressed the daughters’ practice in the mortal layer and, from 1414, drained stolen Practitioner frequency into the containment locks. The Church’s institutional architecture of guilt and compliance was its closest mortal-layer expression. The arc refuses the Wrong Compact explicitly: not by destroying a structure but by replacing it with the New Compact, ratified at WMB12. Refusal does not invalidate the Compact — no one is forced.
Mechanism
The Horde
The Leviathan's extended geological family. Methane wyrms (blue-white fire, three hundred million years old). Rock dragons (twelve-thousand-year sleep, disoriented by interstates). Coal seam entities. The fault line that ruptures in WMB1 was Haagenti's preparation — four decades of systematic industrial extraction along the fault line he identified and weakened. The Horde's emergence through it is disorientation, not invasion. Samyaza mediates. Brian Lee translates.
Mechanism
The Ley Line Network
The seven Warden positions are connected by primary Ley Lines. Three of Ravos's seventeen identified thread locations are at secondary Ley Line nodes between Warden positions — not at the Warden positions themselves but at intersection points where the connections between positions run strongest.
Mechanism
The New Compact's Frequency
The compact moves through the Church's institutional architecture like a key in a lock that has been cut to fit a different door. The architecture that fed the locks on Satan’s soul begins to lose its fuel — replacing the Compact changes systems built to depend on it (WMB11). The decay of the locks accelerates; the later Order crisis is not resolved here. Metatron: the formal framework is downstream of the Web. Always was. The compact makes that explicit.
