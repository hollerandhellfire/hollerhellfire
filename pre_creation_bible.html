THE OLD WORLD

The old world already had politics. Sano’s Creation was never the first world, only the first his hands made.
The Old World
Confirmed
A World Before Sano’s Creation
Sano and Nepisano did not come from nothing. They came from a full pre-Creation world with its own law, its own politics, and its own grudges. Sano’s Bang built a solar system inside that larger universe; it did not begin the universe. The Cycles are fixed to Sano’s Creation only, so the old world was never reset. Even the Keeper of Time has limits, and the edge of her son’s Creation is hers.
Confirmed
A Saga of Its Own
This pre-Creation story is going to be a saga all its own: a journey of righting wrongs and seeking justice for those betrayed. It carries the series thesis: a correct relationship built on trust that is proven, and belief in what is proven true, as the foundation of the verb of Love, choosing each other over and over again no matter the cost, is more powerful than any other force in the universe.
Confirmed
The Order of the Old World
The society works like a monarchy. At the top is the Hearth, the ruling House. Under it is the Concord of Nine, the council, and each Concord seat is the ruling House of one of nine Factions. A Faction is a group of Houses under its ruling House: eighteen member Houses (two per Faction) who govern areas of the universe, then minor Houses (client, craft and broken), the common peoples, and slaves. See The Factions. People who belong to no House at all have no standing and no protection. See The Lower Orders. The hearth currently sits empty.
Confirmed
Two Crimes, a Generation Apart
The old world’s history turns on two crimes about a generation apart. First, the takeover of House Aurelane, the first ruling house, in a night of killing with the paper afterward. Then, about a generation later, the frame of Igren, the ruler the Concord itself had chosen. See The Houses and The First Betrayal.
Confirmed
Where Creation Ends
Sano’s Creation stops at the Oort Cloud, its outermost layer, which is the Abyss: a place where life in any form should not continue. Past it is the Unwritten, the space between galaxies, a layer of absolute darkness that no one honestly knows anything about. That is why it is called the Unwritten. It lies outside Sano’s Creation, and it is the domain of Gamiel, head of the first ruling house, living there in exile.
Confirmed
A Society That Spans the Universe
The old world’s society spreads far and wide throughout the universe. All kinds of races live in it: creatures humans have never heard of, and some that look familiar but go by different names. Each House of the Concord of Nine is a different race, and the eighteen Houses beneath them are different races again, often conquered peoples.
Confirmed
Factions and Founding Factions
In the old world a Faction is a group of Houses under one ruling House, and there are nine of them (see The Factions). They are a separate set from the Founding Factions of Sano’s Creation (Voljeti, Nasilin, Davos and the rest), though the two sets echo each other. The Founding Factions existed before Creation in some form; what each was in the old world is reserved for development.
Confirmed
Slavery
Slavery exists in the old world and the whole Concord depends on it. Slaves come from conquest, debt, birth, and trade across the stars. A slave’s legal standing varies by House, cruel in some and gentler in others. The Hearth was moving to end it. Slavery’s crushed revolts are remembered in whispers (see The Lower Orders).
Confirmed
The Hearth Stands Empty
With Igren imprisoned and Agrea gone, the hearth sits empty. The Concord rules in its place, and Augustus Valentine holds the Concord’s main seat.
Proposed
How the Law Worked
The old world’s load-bearing principle is the one the saga inherits: Reciprocity. Authority was legitimate only while it was owed and returned. The ruling house held that law at the center, and the Concord of Nine advised it from the edge. A world run on relationship is also a world with one catastrophic failure mode: a believable lie about a bond. Slavery is the largest standing violation of that principle.
Proposed
Why It Matters
Reading the old world as a functioning civilization, not a mythic blank, makes the First Betrayal a political crime with real culprits and real beneficiaries. It also explains why the wider universe can still be out there when the signal returns: it never stopped.

THE HOUSES

You are known by your House, even if you are nothing but a servant.
The Houses
Confirmed
Houses and Families
In the old world the society is made of Houses and Families, and Houses gather into Factions under a ruling House. You are known by the House you come from, even if you are not a member and are nothing but a servant. A servant of a House is of that House.
Confirmed
House Aurelane
House Aurelane was the first ruling house. Its family was known far and wide for its smithcraft and for the impossibly beautiful things it made from metals gathered across the universe. Its entire extended House was murdered in a hostile takeover, and the House was struck from the Records in the Archive. Its head was Gamiel.
Confirmed
The Takeover of Aurelane
About a generation before the frame, Aurelane fell in a night of killing with the paper afterward: the murders came first, and a forged order of dissolution covered them. Valentine, Dunmore and Fenwick carried it out, with Fenwick carrying the killers and the cover. House Vesper struck Aurelane from the Records on the forged order, deceived. The Concord then chose House Debry to rule.
Confirmed
Aurelane’s People
The servants and craftsmen of House Aurelane were scattered, and many were enslaved. A struck House has no standing in the Records, so its people had no House to be of and no protection. Some of them still work metal as Aurelane did, in secret (see The Lower Orders).
Confirmed
Gamiel · Head of Aurelane
Gamiel was wounded and left alive on purpose while his entire extended House was killed. Valentine carried him to exile himself, to the Unwritten, where Gamiel has lived for about a generation before the frame. He knows Valentine was behind it, because Valentine told him to his face, and he cannot reach him. He has been waiting for a time when he could. He raised Sano and Nepisano while he waited.
Confirmed
House Valentine · Augustus Valentine
Augustus Valentine leads House Valentine, the House of Trade and Treasury, a House of serpentine aristocrats. He is cold and exact. He is one of the oldest members of the Concord of Nine and the one with the most pull, and he is alive and holds the Concord’s main seat. He is the one responsible for Aurelane’s fall, and he is the same person who betrayed Igren and his family.
Confirmed
Valentine’s Motive and Aim
Valentine wanted Aurelane gone to control what everything is worth: its craft set the standard of value, and he wanted that power for Trade and Treasury. His ultimate aim is the hearth itself.
Confirmed
Valentine and Gamiel
Before the takeover Valentine was Gamiel’s closest friend. He exiled Gamiel instead of killing him because a living exile cannot become a martyr, a political choice and not a mercy. He carried Gamiel to the exile himself and told him the truth to his face there. He believes the darkness he cast his friend into has long since swallowed him.
Confirmed
What Valentine Believes
Valentine is completely unaware that Sano or Nepisano survived. He believes Agrea is dead as well, lost to the same darkness he cast Igren into and exiled his best friend to.
Confirmed
House Obstine · Agrea
Agrea is of House Obstine. Her House sat next to a smaller House, Mooring, and she and Shamain were childhood friends. She fled in grief into the darkness when she believed Igren had betrayed her, and her flight passed close to her sons without her knowing.
Confirmed
House Debry · Igren
Igren is of House Debry, a House that rose after the takeover of Aurelane. The Concord chose Debry to rule. Igren and Agrea rose to rule after the fall, unaware of what it had cost. Igren’s description carries no “Founding Faction” label; Debry is simply a House.
Confirmed
House Mooring · Shamain
Shamain is of House Mooring, a smaller House that had been next to Agrea’s House. He and Agrea were childhood friends. The old echo between Mooring and the first ruling House no longer exists, since that House is now Aurelane.
Confirmed
The Concord’s Houses
The nine ruling Houses of the Concord, each the head of a Faction, have their own tab, and so do the eighteen member Houses beneath them. The Factions tab shows each Faction whole. Valentine, Dunmore, Fenwick, Morrow and Cantor framed Igren. Vesper, Garrow, Tarrant and Linden stayed out.
Proposed
One Hand, Two Wrongs
Reading the two crimes together: the takeover of Aurelane and the frame of Igren are one hand at work, with the same method of erasing a House and taking the trust a ruling family held. Both used a forged paper (the order of dissolution, the false witness) and both used Fenwick’s road. Valentine, Dunmore and Fenwick are the inner three in both crimes, and Morrow and Cantor joined for the second.
Proposed
Safe and Forgettable
The Concord chose Debry as rulers it expected to control. A generation later Igren and Agrea began the reform that threatened every seat, so the Concord’s own choice became the thing it had to destroy.
Proposed
What Valentine’s Hand Implies
Because Valentine carried Gamiel to the Unwritten himself, he knows the way there and where Gamiel’s domain lies. That makes him the strongest candidate for whoever built Igren’s prison in that same domain. Whether he has ever returned, and whether Fenwick’s road was ever used for Gamiel at all, is open.
Proposed
The Archive and the Erasure
Aurelane was struck from the Archive, and canon makes the Bone Mother the keeper and archivist of the Older Mechanism. The Archive of the old world is kept by House Vesper, who struck Aurelane on a forged order. Whether Agrea’s later Archive is the same one is open.
Proposed
Aurelane’s Craft
A House of makers raising two boys, one of whom later builds a Creation. Whether Sano learned making from Gamiel is reserved, but the thread is there: the greatest work of smithcraft the universe ever saw would be a solar system. Aurelane’s scattered smiths still hold its craft in secret.

THE FACTIONS

Nine ruling Houses, nine Factions, and one empty hearth above them all.
The Factions
Confirmed
What a Faction Is
A Faction is a group of Houses under one ruling House. There are nine Factions, one for each seat of the Concord of Nine, and each Concord House is the ruling House of its own Faction. Under the ruling House sit its member Houses (the two vassals), then the client Houses and craft Houses that owe fealty inside that Faction, then the common peoples of its regions, then the slaves its Houses own. A House belongs to the Faction of its open patron; a craft House’s secret ties are listed apart. A Faction is named for its domain, as in the Faction of Trade and Treasury, ruled by House Valentine.
Confirmed
Ranks Inside a Faction
Ruling House (Concord seat) → Member Houses (the two vassals, often conquered peoples) → Client Houses and Craft Houses (minor Houses with standing) → common peoples (free, with hard choices) → slaves. Outside every Faction: broken Houses (listed by number) and the unhoused. Above all nine Factions: the Hearth.
Confirmed
Not the Founding Factions
The nine old-world Factions are a separate set from the Founding Factions of Sano’s Creation (Voljeti, Nasilin, Davos, Arcana, Sjen, Susti, Dzin). The two sets echo each other: the old-world word is the same, and some Creation-era Factions may be what certain old-world Houses were reborn as, but nothing is linked yet. Where the two sets might match is reserved for development.
Confirmed
The Hearth Stands Above the Factions
The Hearth is not a Faction. It sits above all nine as the throne, and its ruling House, Debry (with Obstine, Agrea’s House), stands over every Faction. Mooring, the small House beside Obstine, has no Faction yet (open). Aurelane, the first ruling House, was struck from the Records and belongs to no Faction; its scattered people are among the unhoused.
Confirmed
Faction of Trade and Treasury · Ruled by House Valentine
Ruling House: Valentine (Augustus Valentine, serpentine aristocrats). Its ruling House framed Igren. Member Houses: Pelham, Reynard. Client Houses: Brask, Ingram, Tunstall, Prescott. Craft Houses: Merriweather, Latchford. Secretly served by: Lapidary (open Faction: Metals and Materials). Common peoples: Tessarin.

Confirmed
Faction of Passage and Roads · Ruled by House Fenwick
Ruling House: Fenwick (Sorrel Fenwick, shape-shifters). Its ruling House framed Igren. Member Houses: Carrick, Tidewell. Client Houses: Jessop, Thackeray. Craft Houses: Loomis, Dockery, Pendulum. Secretly served by: Meridian (open Faction: Arms and Wardenship). Common peoples: Skerrin, Hullbacks.
Confirmed
Faction of Rites and Remembrance · Ruled by House Morrow
Ruling House: Morrow (Elias Morrow, ashen-skinned, humanlike people). Its ruling House framed Igren. Member Houses: Bellamy, Wren. Client Houses: Lathe, Penhallow, Calder. Craft Houses: Pendry, Ashlar, Myrrh. Secretly served by: Pendulum (open Faction: Passage and Roads). Common peoples: Candlemen, Witnesses.
Confirmed

Ruling House: Cantor (Dorian Cantor, bat-like, winged listeners). Its ruling House framed Igren. Member Houses: Sennet, Evensong. Client Houses: Quinlan, Thorne. Craft Houses: Lutter, Cadence. Secretly served by: Latchford (open Faction: Trade and Treasury). Common peoples: Gildhands, Wirewalkers.
Confirmed

Ruling House: Vesper (Perpetua Vesper, avian, owl-like watchers). Its ruling House stayed out of the frame. Member Houses: Ellery, Fairchild. Client Houses: Ainsley. Craft Houses: Vellum, Indigo, Matrix. Common peoples: Briars, Queue.
Confirmed
Faction of Arms and Wardenship · Ruled by House Garrow
Ruling House: Garrow (Halvard Garrow, ursine soldiers and wardens). Its ruling House stayed out of the frame. Member Houses: Stillwell, Grimsby. Client Houses: Dravik, Bannock, Radcliffe. Craft Houses: Saddler, Bladewell, Meridian. Secretly served by: Ashlar (open Faction: Rites and Remembrance). Common peoples: Dusk-wives.

Confirmed

THE HEARTH

The marriage joined what the Concord divided. When the bond broke, so did the balance.
The Hearth
Confirmed
Who Held the Hearth, in Order
The hearth has had three stages. First, House Aurelane was the first ruling house. After Aurelane fell, the Concord ruled in the interim. Then the Concord chose House Debry, and Igren and Agrea held the hearth until the frame. Today the hearth sits empty and the Concord rules again. Igren and Agrea were the first rulers after the takeover, never the first rulers.
Confirmed
The Ruling House
Igren and Agrea held the hearth. They were the ruling house of the old world, and the Concord of Nine advised them. They are also the parents of Sano and Nepisano. They rose to rule after the takeover of Aurelane, unaware of it, and the Concord chose House Debry. Today the hearth sits empty and the Concord rules.
Confirmed
The Reform
The Hearth was moving to end slavery. The reform was known only inside the Concord, presented as a proposal and quietly opposed. Because all of the Concord stood to lose from it, it made Igren and Agrea a threat to every seat.
Confirmed
Igren · The Father
Igren is of House Debry, the Bone Mother’s partner, and the boys’ father. He is alive and imprisoned in the Unwritten, the intergalactic dark outside Creation, inside the very domain where Gamiel lives, and Gamiel does not know. He had nothing to do with the attack on his family; he was himself betrayed. He was imprisoned extralegally, before any hearing.
Confirmed
Agrea · The Bone Mother
Agrea is the Bone Mother’s name. She is of House Obstine. She was the Keeper of Time before the frame and became the Master Gatekeeper after it, and she is the only one who will carry the memory of the Cycles. She honestly believes her sons were killed and that Igren was the one who betrayed her and them. She fled in grief into the darkness, passing close to her sons without knowing, and Valentine believes she died there. Her name is a terminal spoiler along with the rest of her origin.
Confirmed
The Hearth Stood Above All Nine
Igren and Agrea together held what the nine each held a piece of: treasury, metals, roads, rites, signal, records, arms, law and healing. The marriage joined what the Concord divided. No single seat could do alone what the Hearth did together.
Proposed
Why the Frame Aimed at the Bond
A Hearth that unites what the Concord divides cannot be overruled by any one seat, only broken apart. That is why the frame went after the bond between Igren and Agrea and not just one of them.
Proposed
Her Silence at the Bang
The existing Bone Mother bible has her silent at the Bang while every faction clamored. In light of this, her silence reads as a mother watching her lost sons wake in a world that no longer knows her, with no way to say so without breaking the Cycles.

THE CONCORD OF NINE

Five framed him. Four stayed out. The court that should have judged him never heard his case.
The Concord of Nine
Confirmed
Nine Houses, Nine Races
The Concord of Nine is nine ruling Houses that act as the council under the Hearth, each the head of its own Faction. They are new and completely separate from the Founding Factions of Sano’s Creation, and so are the nine Factions they rule. Each House is a different race. Each Concord House has two vassal Houses beneath it (see The Eighteen). Five of the nine framed Igren; four stayed out. Today the hearth is empty and the Concord rules.
Confirmed
The Nine at a Glance
Framed Igren:
Valentine · Augustus · Trade and Treasury · serpentine
Dunmore · Gorran · Metals and Materials · squat, mineral-skinned
Fenwick · Sorrel · Passage and Roads · shape-shifters

Linden · Hazel · Life and Healing · plant-like, rooted
Confirmed
The Inner Three
Valentine, Dunmore and Fenwick carried out the takeover of Aurelane a generation before the frame. Morrow and Cantor joined them for the frame. The inner three are the core of the Concord’s guilt.
Confirmed
All of the Concord Stood to Lose
Ending slavery would have cost every seat of the Concord, including the four that stayed out of the frame. Staying out of the frame did not mean supporting the reform.
Confirmed
House Valentine · Trade and Treasury
Faction: Trade and Treasury · Ruling House. Augustus Valentine leads a House of serpentine aristocrats. He is cold and exact, among the Concord’s oldest members, holds its main seat, and has the most pull. He was Gamiel’s closest friend, destroyed Aurelane to control what everything is worth, and betrayed Igren. He wants the hearth itself, and he believes Agrea and the boys are dead. Vassals: Pelham and Reynard.
Confirmed
House Dunmore · Metals and Materials
Faction: Metals and Materials · Ruling House. A House of squat, broad, mineral-skinned people who keep metals and materials. Its head is Gorran Dunmore, blunt and loyal to a bargain, who kept the contracts and asked no questions. Dunmore took part in the takeover of Aurelane and joined the frame. Aurelane’s fall handed it the metal contracts, so it profited from the first crime. Vassals: Blackmoor and Hallam.
Confirmed
House Fenwick · Passage and Roads
Faction: Passage and Roads · Ruling House. A House of shape-shifters who keep the roads. Its head is Sorrel Fenwick, smooth and patient, who treats impersonation as a craft and the frame as a commission. Fenwick took part in the takeover of Aurelane, carrying the killers and the cover, and its price was a monopoly on the roads and ports, including Aurelane’s routes and Carrick’s ports and crossings. It then joined the frame and did both things: its shape-shifters impersonated Igren, wearing his face for the evidence Agrea saw, and they carried him to the Unwritten, passing the border wardens of House Grimsby unseen. Vassals: Carrick and Tidewell.
Confirmed
House Morrow · Rites and Remembrance
Faction: Rites and Remembrance · Ruling House. A House of ashen-skinned, soft-voiced, humanlike people who keep oaths and ceremony. Its head is Elias Morrow, soft-voiced and gentle, who swore the false oath himself and has never broken another. Morrow joined the frame by swearing false witness, so the lie came with the weight of a holy oath. Vassals: Bellamy and Wren.
Confirmed
House Cantor · Signal and Voice
Faction: Signal and Voice · Ruling House. A House of bat-like, winged listeners who keep communication across the stars. Its signal is fast and open: it crosses the worlds in an instant but can be listened to, and Sennet’s relays do exactly that. Sealed word travels by Tidewell’s couriers instead. Its head is Dorian Cantor, proud of how far the story traveled, who thinks of it as his masterwork. Cantor joined the frame by carrying the false story across the universe, so the lie felt true everywhere. Vassals: Sennet and Evensong.
Confirmed
House Vesper · Records and Memory
Faction: Records and Memory · Ruling House. A House of avian, owl-like watchers who keep the Archive. Its head is Perpetua Vesper, meticulous, who has never forgotten an order. Vesper was deceived. After the killing it struck Aurelane from the Records on a forged order of dissolution, and carried out the erasure without knowing it was a murder cover-up. Vassals: Ellery and Fairchild.
Confirmed
The Master Ledger
The Master Ledger lies in the deepest vaults of Vesper’s Archive, behind the one lock Latchford’s key cannot open. It is an enchanted book that acts as a living record. It records all history on its own, and in recent history it learned to communicate with its keepers. No one knows who made it; it was already old when the Archive was built. Its keepers are Vesper and its vassal Ellery. It does not speak plainly: it changes what it shows each reader. Over time it asked three things. First, why a House was missing from its pages. Next, why the Hearth’s record was incomplete and where the missing children were. Last, it flatly refused to record the frame as true and held a long, foul session about the framing and the lies. Whether it still holds Aurelane, and whether Perpetua Vesper, Imogen Ellery or anyone in the Concord knows what it has said, are open.
Confirmed
House Garrow · Arms and Wardenship
Faction: Arms and Wardenship · Ruling House. A House of ursine, enormous soldiers and wardens. Its head is Halvard Garrow, an honorable old soldier, loyal to the Hearth and uneasy in the Concord. Garrow stayed out and is loyal. The attack on Shamain and the boys was carried out by hired outsiders with no House, not by Garrow’s soldiers. Garrow’s garrisons did, however, crush the old revolts against slavery (see The Lower Orders). Vassals: Stillwell and Grimsby.
Confirmed
House Tarrant · Law and Judgment
Faction: Law and Judgment · Ruling House. A House of feline, lynx-like, impartial judges. Its head is Vivienne Tarrant, a fierce reformer who has long suspected the Concord bends the law. Tarrant stayed out and is loyal. Valentine had the Concord declare Igren guilty by emergency council order, with no trial, so Tarrant’s court was bypassed. Vivienne suspects the order was irregular but has no proof. Vassals: Voss and Hargrove.
Confirmed
House Linden · Life and Healing
Faction: Life and Healing · Ruling House. A House of plant-like, rooted, green-skinned healers. Its head is Hazel Linden, brisk and practical, who runs the great hospitals and has no time for politics. Linden stayed out of the frame. Vassals: Brandt and Wickham.
Proposed
The Loyal Houses Are Not Clean
All nine seats depended on slavery, so Garrow’s garrisons, Tarrant’s courts, Linden’s hospitals and Vesper’s registries all rested on it. The four that stayed out are loyal to the Hearth and to the law, not free of the system. That gives the saga a harder question than who is guilty: whether the loyal Houses will give up what they depend on to do the right thing.
Proposed
What They Gained
Advisors who frame the ruler gain proximity without accountability. With the hearth empty, the Concord became the working authority of the old world, and Valentine’s main seat is where the real power sits. The frame also killed the reform. Nothing in the Concord stops him from simply taking the hearth except the nine seats themselves, which is why he needs the others and why the four who stayed out matter.
Proposed
The Four Who Stayed Out
Vesper, Garrow, Tarrant and Linden are the story’s hinge for reconnection. Vesper’s Archive may still hold the forged order, and its vassal Ellery keeps private copies of what it was told to destroy. Tarrant’s court never heard Igren’s case, and its vassal Voss keeps an unofficial file on it. Garrow’s soldiers are the strength a loyal side would need. Linden’s midwife House delivered the boys. Whether each Head knows Igren is alive is open.
Proposed
The Hired Hands
Someone hired the outsiders who killed Shamain, and a night of killing a generation earlier probably used the same kind of hands. The likeliest hand is Valentine’s, with Trade and Treasury paying for what the Concord could not do openly. House Reynard, which handles debt and collection, is a possible source of unnamed hands, though its knowledge of the frame is open.

THE EIGHTEEN

Under every seat of the Concord, two conquered peoples hold the world’s lands, and each one remembers being conquered.
The Eighteen
Confirmed
How the Eighteen Work
Under each Concord House are two vassal Houses, eighteen in all, which are the member Houses of its Faction. Each one is a different race, often a conquered people, with its own sub-specialty within its Concord House’s domain, and each governs areas of the universe. Under the eighteen are minor Houses, common peoples, and slaves.
Confirmed
Build Status
All eighteen are built. Valentine (Pelham, Reynard) · Dunmore (Blackmoor, Hallam) · Fenwick (Carrick, Tidewell) · Cantor (Sennet, Evensong) · Morrow (Bellamy, Wren) · Vesper (Ellery, Fairchild) · Garrow (Stillwell, Grimsby) · Tarrant (Voss, Hargrove) · Linden (Brandt, Wickham).
Confirmed
House Pelham · Credit and Banking
Faction: Trade and Treasury · Member House. Vassal of Valentine. A conquered people of tall, multi-limbed weavers, spider-like. Its head is Dame Tamsin Pelham, loyal to Valentine to the bone because he saved her House from being broken. It governs a rich core region. Pelham knows about the frame and keeps silent.
Confirmed
House Reynard · Debt and Collection
Faction: Trade and Treasury · Member House. Vassal of Valentine. A conquered people of fox-like, quick beings. Its head is Master Corbin, young, ambitious, and hungry to prove himself to Valentine. It governs a ring of mining worlds in debt. Whether Reynard knows about the frame is open.
Confirmed
House Blackmoor · Rare and Precious Materials
Faction: Metals and Materials · Member House. Vassal of Dunmore. A conquered people of salamander-like heat-dwellers. Its head is Vesna Blackmoor, cunning, who hoards secrets as carefully as gems. It governs a chain of volcanic worlds.
Confirmed
House Hallam · Salvage and Scrap
